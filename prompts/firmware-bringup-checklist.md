# Firmware bring-up checklist

## Goal
Create a bench-first Pico 2 firmware bring-up checklist that can be used before any field trial.

## Required output
- Numbered bench steps.
- One pass criterion per step.
- Required tools or instruments.
- Expected logs or measurements.
- Stop conditions that require owner review.

## Constraints
- Do not invent hardware that is not in the input files.
- Do not claim firmware is already running on hardware.
- Keep the checklist under 30 lines.

## Tone
Practical and safety-conscious.
