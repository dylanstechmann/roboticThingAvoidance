# Organizer Q&A draft

## Goal
Turn the current QA pack into concise, organizer-facing questions that can be posted to the official Q&A.

## Required output
- One numbered question per unresolved QA item.
- No more than 5 sentences per question.
- Plain language, no internal branch names.
- Cite the manual section or line when the input provides it.
- End with a short “What we need answered” sentence.

## Constraints
- Do not invent organizer answers.
- Do not claim owner review or physical testing.
- Do not introduce new rule interpretations.

## Tone
Respectful, specific, and easy for a non-project reader to understand.
