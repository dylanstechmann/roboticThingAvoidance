# CAD fit review

## Goal
Review the mechanical fit evidence, especially the 7.8 mm motor-body gap failure, and propose concrete measurement or redesign checks.

## Required output
1. Restate the failure in one sentence.
2. List the two or three most useful next physical measurements.
3. Identify any CAD or assembly assumptions that should be rechecked.
4. Recommend whether the current design is safe to fabricate as-is.

## Constraints
- Use only the supplied evidence.
- Do not invent new dimensions.
- Do not claim the robot is physically validated.
- Mark every recommendation as advisory.

## Tone
Engineering-review style, concise, evidence-first.
