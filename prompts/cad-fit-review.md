# CAD fit review

## Goal
Plan concrete caliper measurements of the received drive motor against its published dimensions, to confirm or refute the historical 7.8 mm motor-body gap finding (from the archived v13 vendor-geometry study) before any layout revision.

## Required output
1. Restate the historical gap finding in one sentence, marked as a result derived from archived vendor geometry, not current authority.
2. List the two or three most useful next physical measurements, each with the tool, the datum, and the expected value from the published dimensions.
3. Identify any assembly assumptions that should be rechecked on the received parts.
4. Recommend whether the current design is safe to fabricate as-is.

## Constraints
- Use only the supplied evidence: the published manufacturer dimensions and the bench kit context.
- Do not invent new dimensions.
- Vendor CAD is not used in this project. Do not seek or reference manufacturer CAD files; the 7.8 mm figure is a historical finding, not current authority.
- Do not claim the robot is physically validated.
- Mark every recommendation as advisory.

## Tone
Engineering-review style, concise, evidence-first.
