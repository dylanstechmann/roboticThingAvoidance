# Vendor CAD research

Find vendor references for the drive motors recorded in the input files: product pages, dimension drawings, STEP or CAD files, and any vendor-published torque or current specs.

Report:

1. the vendor page or file URL for each finding;
2. which motor variant it applies to;
3. the dimensions that matter to the 7.8 mm motor-body gap question;
4. whether a downloadable CAD model exists.

In live mode the harness sends the recorded query to the search provider and stores the formatted results as the evidence body; this template records the intent and the review questions.

## Advisory boundary

This output is agent-run and advisory. Do not claim owner review, physical testing, or competition qualification.
