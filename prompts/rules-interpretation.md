# Rules interpretation cross-check

## Goal
Cross-check the current rules interpretations against the supplied evidence and identify contradictions or unverified assumptions.

## Required output
- A short table with:
  - interpretation
  - evidence path
  - confidence
  - next verification step
- A list of any contradictions.
- A list of questions that still need an organizer answer.

## Constraints
- Do not invent new rules.
- Do not claim organizer rulings.
- Do not claim owner review or physical testing.
- Keep the output under 50 lines.

## Tone
Neutral, evidence-based, and suitable for an engineering review.
