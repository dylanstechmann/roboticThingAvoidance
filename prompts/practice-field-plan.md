# Practice-field plan

## Goal
Draft a practical build and measurement plan for the competition practice field.

## Required output
1. Materials and dimensions to confirm before cutting.
2. Build order.
3. Measurement checklist with tolerances.
4. Photo or log requirements for evidence.
5. First field-test criteria.

## Constraints
- Use the current backcast and mechanical-readiness evidence.
- Do not invent organizer field dimensions.
- Do not claim any physical work has already happened.
- Keep the plan under 40 lines.

## Tone
Build-instruction style, concise, and directly executable by the owner.
