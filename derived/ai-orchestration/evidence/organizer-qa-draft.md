### Question 1: Minimum robot size geometry and contraction (QA01)
Rule R01 in Game Manual 1 V3.0 (line 242) states that the robot must have a minimum size of 10 inches long by 10 inches wide for 3 inches up from the surface. In GM1 V2.0 (line 234), R01 also stated that the robot may not contract this portion at any time, but that sentence was omitted in V3.0. We would like to clarify if a circular chassis with a 10-inch diameter satisfies the 10-by-10-inch requirement, and whether the lower 10-by-10-inch volume must be maintained continuously throughout the match.
**What we need answered:** Does a 10-inch circular footprint satisfy R01, and is expansion or contraction of that lower volume permitted during a match?

### Question 2: AprilTag mounting location and post-randomization settings (QA02)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 §3.3 (line 170) states that the AprilTag is mounted to the wall separating the pit area from the racecourse, whereas §3.4.1 (line 174) states it is fastened to the wall by the starting area. Furthermore, §3.4.1 (line 179) notes the Technician declares all settings are selected before match start, while Rule GS01 (line 212) prohibits field interaction after randomization. We would like to verify the exact mounting location, height, and facing of the AprilTag, and whether teams may set an onboard selector switch after randomization without violating GS01.
**What we need answered:** Where exactly is the AprilTag positioned relative to the start box, and can a team set an onboard switch after randomization occurs?

### Question 3: Obstacle vehicle specifications and placement rules (QA03)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 §3.1, §3.4.1, Appendix B, and Appendix E (lines 127, 175, 321, 379) describe three randomly placed toy vehicle obstacles set in quadrants across inner and outer lanes. Appendix E indicates quadrants start 2 inches from the side rail and stop 2 inches from the center rail for a 16-inch total width, though the physical lane spacing is roughly 22 inches. We would like clarification on the specific model dimensions, allowed orientations within the quadrant, whether two vehicles can occupy the same straightaway, and which rail faces the 2-inch offsets reference.
**What we need answered:** What are the exact toy obstacle dimensions, valid quadrant orientations/lane splits, and measuring reference faces?

### Question 4: Driving across white track boundary lines (QA04)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 §3.2 (line 131) defines the racecourse and pit areas, but the track contains painted white lines where end strips measure narrower than the 10-inch minimum robot width required by GM1 V3.0 R01 (line 242). We would like to verify whether crossing or straddling the white painted lines on the track surface is permitted.
**What we need answered:** Are robots required to stay within painted lane lines, or are travel paths constrained only by physical walls and dividers?

### Question 5: Pit area access openings and routing (QA05)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 Appendix G (lines 390, 393) describes entering the pit area for the tire exchange challenge, but does not specify which checkered boundary openings may be traversed. We want to confirm whether a robot may enter and exit through the same checkered opening, whether either end of the dividing wall is valid, and if crossing the starting box area during pit transit is allowed.
**What we need answered:** Which checkered openings are legal for entering and exiting the pit area, and is using the same opening for both entry and exit permitted?

### Question 6: Lap progression, direction, and final lap definition (QA06)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 §3.1 and §3.2.2 (lines 127, 149–150) require completing three laps, making a pit stop between laps, and raising a school flag on the final victory lap. Because the pit detour can cross the start/finish area multiple times, we would like to know how lap progression is officially scored, whether reversing or driving clockwise is permitted, and the exact boundary where the final lap begins for raising the flag.
**What we need answered:** Is travel direction restricted, how are laps counted during pit entry/exit crossings, and at what point does the final victory lap officially start?

### Question 7: Pit box entry requirement and match finish timing (QA07)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 Appendix G (line 392) states that the robot itself does not have to enter the assigned pit box, but the sequence on line 393 states "enter assigned Pit Box," while §3.3 (line 160) requires more than 75% of the robot to be in the box for points. Additionally, §3.6 (line 222) awards points for crossing the finish line in 3 minutes or less. We would like to clarify if a robot must physically enter the box or may reach in to swap tires, whether a pit stop is required to earn the 3-minute finish points, and whether the time limit stops at the finish-line crossing or upon parking in the pit box.
**What we need answered:** Must the robot body enter the pit box, is a pit stop mandatory for completion points, and where is the official match end time recorded?

### Question 8: Start flag signaling and Appendix F release (QA08)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 §3.1 (line 127) states the robot begins when the green flag is lowered, but §3.4.1 and §3.4.2 (lines 176, 181) state the flag is held secured down and raised by the referee to start the match. Furthermore, Appendix F (line 383) indicates mechanical details will be provided in Version 3.1. We would like to confirm whether the start signal is a flag raise or lower, where the flag mechanism is positioned, and when Version 3.1 will be published.
**What we need answered:** Is the match start signaled by raising or lowering the green flag, where is it located, and when will Appendix F specifications be available?

### Question 9: Field construction tolerances and wall dimensions (QA09)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 Rule G02 (line 202) provides a positional tolerance of +/- 1 inch for field elements. We would like to confirm whether this tolerance applies independently to opposing walls (potentially narrowing a track corridor by up to 2 inches) and whether perimeter and divider walls use nominal 1-inch lumber (actual 3/4-inch thickness) or full 1.0-inch material.
**What we need answered:** Does the +/- 1 inch tolerance apply independently per wall element, and what is the exact physical thickness of the timber walls?

### Question 10: Team Game Elements and publicity award scoring (QA10)
Game Manual 2 V3.0 Rule TE01 (line 214) provides inspection rules for Team Game Elements, but no team-supplied field elements are described elsewhere in the competition manual. Additionally, §3.6 (line 244) awards 10 points if the team enters the publicity contest. We would like to clarify whether teams are expected or permitted to supply any physical Team Game Elements, and whether publicity points are credited once to the overall standings or added to each individual match score.
**What we need answered:** Are Team Game Elements used in this challenge, and are publicity points awarded per match or once overall?
